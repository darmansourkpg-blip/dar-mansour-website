Most guides tell you which hotel to book. We'd start one step earlier: on Koh Phangan, **choosing the right area matters more than choosing the right hotel.** The island is small but surprisingly varied — a wellness village, a sunset coast, a luxury bay, a party beach and quiet fishing villages are all within a short scooter ride, and each gives you a completely different trip.

**In brief:** stay in **Sri Thanu** for wellness, **Hin Kong** for sunsets and food, **Thong Nai Pan** for quiet luxury, **Haad Rin / Baan Tai** for nightlife, and **Haad Yao / Haad Salad** for the beach. Below, how to choose your area — then the best-rated hotels, resorts and villas in each, with where to eat nearby.

## Where to Stay, Area by Area

### Sri Thanu — Wellness & Food
Koh Phangan's wellness village, and one of its best areas for cafés, healthy cuisine and international restaurants — an easy, relaxed base on the west coast, close to sunset beaches and to Dar Mansour.

#### Benjamin's Hut
A female-owned beachfront bungalow resort by Zen Beach, with direct white-sand beach access, free kayaks and snorkelling gear, a pool and a restaurant — relaxed, quiet and welcoming, a short walk from Sri Thanu's cafés and massages.

**Best for —** Beach · Couples · Value<br>
**Price —** ฿฿<br>
**Guest ratings —** Agoda 9.0 · Google 4.7<br>
**Location —** Sri Thanu (Zen Beach). [View on map ↗](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KjFjyQ3yEz5sQta98)

#### Bliss Villas
Three boutique beachfront pool villas — elegant and modern — set on a secluded 12,000 m² beachfront plot, with crystal-clear water, white sand and some of the island's finest sunsets.

**Best for —** Luxury villas · Couples · Privacy<br>
**Price —** ฿฿฿<br>
**Guest ratings —** Agoda 9.5 · Google 4.8<br>
**Location —** Sri Thanu Beach. [View on map ↗](https://maps.app.goo.gl/D7nzekqJFF5e3zte6)

**Where to eat nearby →** see our [Sri Thanu food guide](journal-where-to-eat-sri-thanu.html). A short ride away, [Dar Mansour](moroccan-menu-koh-phangan.html) serves slow-cooked Moroccan dinners.

### Hin Kong & Nai Wok — Sunsets & the Best Food
Our own stretch of the west coast: relaxed, scenic and home to some of the island's best sunset restaurants — and just minutes from Dar Mansour.

#### Kupu Kupu Phangan Beach Villas & Spa by L'Occitane
Private pool villas with a Balinese-influenced Thai design on a quiet stretch of white-sand beach in Nai Wok, between Thong Sala and Hin Kong. Direct beach access, an infinity pool, sunset views over the Gulf, and a Spa by L'Occitane.

**Best for —** Luxury · Couples · Spa<br>
**Price —** ฿฿฿<br>
**Guest ratings —** Agoda 8.9 · Google 4.8<br>
**Location —** Nai Wok, between Thong Sala and Hin Kong. [View on map ↗](https://share.google/FqsZv939W85kABhRu)

#### Joy Beach Villas
A beachfront collection of 13 exclusive villas on the west coast, with direct beach access, pools and tropical gardens — a 10–15 minute drive from Thong Sala Pier, and walking distance to west-coast favourites.

**Best for —** Couples · Families · Beachfront<br>
**Price —** ฿฿<br>
**Guest ratings —** Agoda 9.2 · Google 4.9<br>
**Location —** Hin Kong / Sri Thanu coast. [View on map ↗](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LSCiRrgx7RUT1UhB6)

An intimate adults-only retreat of private boutique bungalows built with local Thai craftsmanship, each opening onto a tropical garden with the beach and pool just outside. Barefoot luxury, privacy and one of the island's highest guest scores.

### Thong Nai Pan — Quiet Luxury
A pair of sheltered bays on the east coast, and the island's home of quiet, upscale resorts — beautiful beaches, calmer water and some of Koh Phangan's highest-rated stays.

#### Buri Rasa Village Phangan
A boutique beachfront resort on Thong Nai Pan Noi, blending traditional Thai architecture with contemporary design and highly personal service. All-suite rooms (many with pool or gulf views), a beachfront restaurant, an outdoor pool and activities from kayaking to diving.

**Best for —** Couples · Families · Relaxed luxury<br>

#### Santhiya Koh Phangan Resort & Spa
An eco-luxury teakwood resort on Thong Nai Pan Noi beach, known for carved-teak detailing, private pool villas and direct beach access. Gulf-view rooms, a waterfront restaurant and bar, a spa, and yoga, cooking classes and kayaking.

## Practical Tips

- **Pick the area, then the hotel.** Decide what kind of trip you want — wellness, sunsets, luxury, nightlife or the beach — and choose the area first.
- **Book ahead in high season.** December to March and every Full Moon Party fill the best-rated places quickly.
- **Rent a scooter.** The island is small; a scooter turns "far" into ten minutes and opens up every beach and restaurant.
- **Check current ratings and hours.** Guest scores and opening times shift through the year — confirm on Google or Agoda before booking.

## Final Thoughts

Koh Phangan rewards travellers who choose their base with intention. Whether you want to wake up to yoga in Sri Thanu, watch the sun set over Hin Kong, retreat to a quiet Thong Nai Pan bay or dance through a Full Moon in Haad Rin, there's an area — and a place to stay — that fits. And wherever you end up, you're never far from a memorable meal.
