How We Think About Hospitality

People don't remember floor plans.

They remember how a place made them feel. The table they wanted to sit at. The light at dinner. The music. The wall they photographed. The detail they told someone about the next day.

That's where hospitality becomes memorable — and it's designed on purpose.

Hospitality in Practice

Dar Mansour

A restaurant designed as a complete world.

For Dar Mansour in Koh Phangan, Eden & Beyond shaped the restaurant across interiors, furniture, lighting, dressed walls, artworks and atmosphere. Nothing was treated as an isolated design decision — every element contributes to the same experience.
